## Your Core Transformation

I help **underground electronic producers with a folder full of unreleased tracks** go from **"one more track, still no release"** to **a licensable, brand-grade catalog entry shipped to DSPs and sync libraries in 21 days** — using the **SIGNAL Method™** (Select → Isolate → Ghost-Master → Narrate → Anchor → License).

The five moves, named so they can't be copied cleanly:

1. **Select** — score the folder with a 12-point OCD Signal Rubric; kill 90% before you touch a DAW.
2. **Isolate** — strip the surviving track to its obsession (the one motif the producer actually can't stop making).
3. **Ghost-Master** — apply the Neural Ghost mastering chain (reference presets + LUFS/true-peak targets tuned for experimental low-end).
4. **Narrate** — generate the release narrative, art brief, and one-page press kit from a filled-in template.
5. **Anchor** — deliver to DSPs + a curated sync/licensing shortlist with pre-negotiated metadata.

Only step 3 touches craft. Everything else is a filled-in form — which is exactly why it productizes.

## Distribution Channel (match existing platform, don't start from zero)

**Primary: the OCD Records submission funnel itself.**

You already have producers submitting tracks. That's the highest-intent audience on the planet for this product — they've *literally* raised their hand asking you to hear them out.

**The play:**

1. Every submission that scores below the "sign this artist" threshold (which is ~99% of them) gets an auto-response through the submission form: *"We're not signing this, but here's why — and here's the same system we used to decide, in kit form."*
2. Link to the sales page. The rejection *is* the marketing.
3. Secondary: post one "kill list" teardown per week on the OCD Records Twitter/IG using rejected-with-permission submissions. Every teardown ends with the kit CTA.

Why this beats "start a TikTok": your submission inbox is a self-refilling lead list of exactly the buyer this product is designed for. Zero new platform. Zero cold audience. The rejection queue you're already sitting on is the distribution engine.
