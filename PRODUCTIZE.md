## Winning Product Structure

### Name
**The Obsession Protocol™: Bedroom to Catalog in 90 Days**

Named after the OCD tagline ("Where Obsession Becomes Art") so it inherits the label's positioning instead of competing with it. The ™ signals proprietary framework — the rule against generic courses.

### Contents
- **Module 0 — The Rubric** (20 min): the 12-point release-readiness scorecard OCD uses internally. This is the shareable artifact; giving it away in the free lead magnet is how the paid product gets discovered.
- **Module 1 — Signal** (4 lessons, ~90 min): stripping demos to their irreducible idea. Includes 3 anonymized teardowns from real submissions.
- **Module 2 — Sharpen** (5 lessons, ~2 hr): mix-headroom targets, sonic-identity scoring, the neural-precision pass. Includes the analog chain reference (Neve/SSL settings actually used in the OCD studios pages).
- **Module 3 — Sign-Off** (3 lessons, ~90 min): mastering chain, LUFS targets by platform, catalog-benchmark A/B protocol.
- **Module 4 — Ship** (4 lessons, ~2 hr): split-sheet template, sync-ready stem export spec, the exact one-sheet format sync supervisors will actually open. This is the moat — most courses stop at "release your music"; this ships them into revenue.
- **The Vault**: 11 downloadable templates — split sheet, one-sheet, stem-delivery README, LUFS target sheet, submission cold-open email, licensing follow-up sequence, scorecard PDF (fillable), master-bus reference chain, session prep checklist, catalog-benchmark A/B worksheet, rejection-to-referral script.
- **Bonus: Rejection Reversal** — the exact template of the "here's what we'd need to see" email OCD sends rejected submissions. Turns rejection into a repeatable improvement loop.

### Delivery mechanism (works at 3am, no operator online)
- Hosted on **Podia or Gumroad** (both handle checkout, delivery, EU VAT, tax, and drip release automatically).
- Purchase → automated welcome email → immediate access → 4-email onboarding sequence over 14 days → module-completion nudges → day-30 upsell to Frequencies community when it exists.
- **Zero live touchpoints required.** Q&A handled by a monthly recorded office-hour uploaded to the members area (not live), so it stays evergreen.

### What makes it irreplaceable
1. **Proof-of-work moat**: every teardown, template, and benchmark comes from real OCD catalog releases — competitors selling "how to release music" can't produce artist rosters, studio specs, or licensing splits as evidence.
2. **Named framework**: the Obsession Protocol becomes the vocabulary. Once artists talk about "running the Sharpen pass," the label's language is the industry's language.
3. **The Ship module**: nobody teaches the last-mile (sync-ready stems + one-sheet format supervisors accept). This is what makes the price defensible and the reviews strong.

### Price point
**$299 launch tier → $499 evergreen** (with $99 rubric-only "lite" as a ladder step).

Rationale: $299 matches the studio's entry tier already published on the site — anchoring the Vault to a familiar OCD price point avoids the "too cheap to be OCD / too expensive for a course" trap. $499 evergreen positions it against the $1,200 next-tier studio session while still being a fraction of that. The $99 lite creates a 3:1 ladder and captures the buyers who bounce at $299.

## Distribution channel (matches existing platform)

**The submission funnel is the channel — don't start a new one.**

`submit.html` is already the highest-intent traffic on the site: producers who took the time to package their work and click "Submit." OCD signs a tiny fraction; the rest currently get silence or a form rejection.

Wire the Vault into that existing flow:
- **On the submit page**: below the form, add the free Rubric PDF ("Score your track before you submit — the same 12 points our A&R uses"). Captures email, adds to Vault email sequence.
- **In the rejection email**: convert the current no-reply into a warm "here's why, and here's the protocol that would get you to yes" — link to the Vault at $299 launch price.
- **On every release page**: add a small "produced using the Obsession Protocol — learn it" callout. Turns the catalog into a permanent product-showcase without an extra content channel.

This uses only surfaces the operator already owns and already updates. No new platform, no cold audience, no algorithm dependency.

Secondary (already in the footer, so free): a single Instagram Reel per new release breaking down which stage of the Protocol shaped it. Content byproduct of shipping releases, not a separate content job.
