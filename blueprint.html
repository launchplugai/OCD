// 04 The Named Method

The OTA Protocol.
Five Phases.

Obsession-to-Artifact. Each phase produces a named artifact; you cannot advance without it. This is the proprietary mechanism.

Phase 01

Signal

Raw capture discipline. Gain-staging, sample rate lock, dead-room reference.

Artifact: Signal Sheet

Phase 02

Structure

Arrangement passes the 32-bar test. Every section earns its place or gets cut.

Artifact: Arrangement Map

Phase 03

Space

Depth, width, and tonal balance built to reference against OCD stems.

Artifact: Spatial Print

Phase 04

Saturation

Harmonic character committed. The mix stops being polite and becomes a statement.

Artifact: Character Bounce

Phase 05

Certification

Pass the 5-Frequency scorecard at 85+. Metadata, ISRC, and delivery masters generated.

Artifact: OCD Certified Master
