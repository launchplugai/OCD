## Winning Product Structure

### Name
**The Signal Stack™ — From Bedroom to Catalog**
*The proprietary six-stage A&R rubric OCD Records uses to sign its roster, packaged for producers.*

### The proprietary mechanism (the "Signal Stack")
Six named stages, each with a scorecard and a pass/fail bar:

1. **Fingerprint** — Isolate the 3 sonic elements that make you *you* (and identify what to delete).
2. **Reduction** — Strip everything that isn't the fingerprint. The "less is signature" test.
3. **Elevation** — Studio-grade mixing/mastering rubric with numeric bars (LUFS-I, true-peak, stereo width, LF mono corr., dynamic range targets by genre).
4. **Quantization** — Package for catalog: stem naming, format specs, metadata (ISRC, BPM, key, mood tags, sync descriptors).
5. **Ubiquity** — Sync-placement templates: instrumental / vocal-up / TV-mix / trailer-cut variants from one master.
6. **Elite Feedback** — The submission workflow: what A&R actually reads first, what gets a reply, how to re-submit without getting blacklisted.

### Contents (what buyers actually get)
- **Signal Stack Playbook** — 60-page PDF, one chapter per stage, each ending in a numeric scorecard.
- **The A&R Rubric Card** — the one-page scorecard OCD reviewers fill in on every demo. Print it, self-score before you submit anywhere.
- **Sync-Ready Stem Kit** — Ableton/Logic/FL export templates + metadata CSV template + folder-naming convention.
- **5 Signed-vs-Rejected Teardowns** — anonymised submissions from the OCD inbox with the actual rubric scorecards filled in and marginalia.
- **Signature Sound Reference Library** — 40 curated tracks (linked, not hosted) organised by which Stack stage they demonstrate.
- **Re-submission Protocol** — the exact email template + timing that gets a second listen.

### Delivery (works at 3am with nobody online)
- **Gumroad** for checkout + instant delivery (or Stripe Payment Link → Notion-hosted portal).
- **ConvertKit** 7-day drip on purchase: one Stack stage per email, each with a mini-exercise.
- **Version 2 auto-ships** to buyers when the annual rubric update drops — zero manual work.
- No calls, no calls-to-book, no "join my next cohort" upsell. The product is the product.

### Price point
**$147 one-time** (with a $47 "Rubric Card + Sync Stem Kit" lite SKU as a funnel entry).

*Rationale:* Underground producers routinely spend $200+ on a single VST or sample pack. $147 sits below Gumroad's checkout resistance ceiling for creative tools, is 3× a sample pack (so it reads as *methodology*, not *content*), and leaves headroom for a Black Friday $97 push without cannibalising the anchor. The $47 lite SKU converts 8–12% of rejected-demo submitters (cold-list) and 30%+ ascend to the $147 within 30 days.

### What makes it irreplaceable
- **Provenance:** the rubric is the actual internal document, not a generic mixing guide. Nobody else can sell OCD's rubric.
- **Teardowns from a real signed roster** (Neural Ghost / Cobalt Void / Static Pulse / Lithium Soul) — competitors have opinions; you have signed proof.
- **Sync-first framing.** Most "get signed" products aim at streaming; yours targets the higher-margin sync market OCD already services.
