## Your Core Transformation

I help obsessive bedroom producers go from **"a hard drive of
unreleased tracks nobody will ever hear"** to **a sync-licensable,
label-ready four-track EP that places on DSPs, sync libraries and
A&R desks in 30 days** — using **The Obsession Loop™** (Obsess →
Compress → Deliver).

The Loop is the proprietary mechanism:

1. **Obsess** — forge a one-sentence sonic identity thesis
   ("Sound Signature") the artist can defend to any A&R in 20 seconds.
2. **Compress** — collapse a year of unreleased experiments into four
   release-grade tracks using the **Signal Stack** (stems, metadata,
   visual identity, sync-ready masters, pitch deck).
3. **Deliver** — place through the **Six-Door Distribution**: DSPs,
   sync libraries, blogs, licensable catalogs, label demo channels,
   direct-to-fan drop.

## Winning Product Structure — **The Obsession Codex**

- **Name:** The Obsession Codex — *The Obsession Loop™ for bedroom
  producers who want to stop hoarding tracks and start getting placed.*

- **Contents (what's inside):**
  - **Part I — Obsess:** Sound Signature worksheet; 40-prompt identity
    interrogation; the "20-second A&R pitch" script.
  - **Part II — Compress:** The Signal Stack template vault — stems
    naming convention, sync-ready mastering spec (LUFS/true-peak
    targets per platform), metadata CSV with ISRC/ISWC fields,
    visual-identity one-pager, 1-page pitch deck in Figma.
  - **Part III — Deliver:** The Six-Door Distribution map —
    pre-populated contact sheets for 40 sync libraries, 25 DSP
    playlist editors, 30 blogs, 15 label demo inboxes, with scripted
    outreach for each door.
  - **The Codex Notion OS:** duplicatable workspace that runs the
    whole 30-day release cycle — Kanban board, daily standup
    template, calendar, revenue tracker.
  - **Seven 5-minute Loom walkthroughs** — one per deliverable.
  - **Bonus:** the "Neural Ghost Autopsy" — a line-by-line teardown
    of how a flagship OCD release was built, mastered, and placed
    (irreplaceable because nobody else has access to the actual
    label's internal files).

- **What makes it irreplaceable:**
  1. The Six-Door contact sheets are hand-built from OCD's working
     Rolodex — no other playbook has verified inboxes.
  2. The Neural Ghost Autopsy is literal label IP; it cannot be
     cloned from YouTube.
  3. The Signal Stack mastering spec is tuned to current sync-agent
     delivery requirements, not generic "release-ready" advice.

- **Delivery (reaches buyers without you):**
  Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy checkout → automated email with Notion
  duplicate link + Google Drive folder of the Stack → Loom playlist
  unlocked after purchase. Zero human touch required. Refund handled
  by platform.

- **Price point:** **$297** one-time, with a **$97 "Lite"** tier
  (Codex + Stack, no Autopsy, no Notion OS) for price-sensitive
  producers.
  Rationale: anchors below a $500 "label coaching" price point but
  stays premium enough to filter tourists. Lite tier captures the
  Submit-page traffic that bounces at $297. Target conversion: 2%
  of submit-page visitors × 500 visitors/mo = 10 sales/mo × $200
  blended AOV = **~$2,000 MRR** by month 2, scaling with traffic.

## Distribution Channel (matches current platform)

**The Submit page itself.** Today the submit funnel accepts demos and
drops them into A&R. Convert it into a two-door intake:

- **Door A — Submit (free):** unchanged for serious artists.
- **Door B — "Not ready yet? Get the Codex."** Soft-sell CTA below
  the submit form for artists who self-identify as not label-ready.

Every artist who lands on `submit.html` is pre-qualified: they're a
bedroom producer looking for a path forward. That's the exact ICP for
the Codex. No new channel required; just route the 90% who won't get
signed into a product that still serves them — and generates revenue
that funds the 10% who do.

Secondary placement: a Codex teaser block on `artist.html` ("How
Neural Ghost built this release — read the autopsy") and on
`licensing.html` ("Want your tracks here? Start with the Codex").

  all fail the "works at 3am without me" test.

  no proprietary contact sheet, commoditized.

  zero on a new channel" rule. The OCD site already attracts the
  exact ICP.
