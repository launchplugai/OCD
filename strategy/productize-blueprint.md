**Neural Ghost Protocol™** — *The OCD Records starter kit for releasing
your first label-grade ghost artist in 30 days.*

The named mechanism is the four-phase **Obsession → Architecture → Ghost
→ Drop** loop. The kit is the artifact form of the protocol.

```

        |
```

Nothing in this pipeline touches the founder until a co-sign submission
arrives, and co-signs are batched async monthly.

## Distribution Channel (matches existing platform)

**The OCD Records submission form itself.**

The label already receives unsolicited submissions through `submit.html`.
~95%+ are rejected. Today they get silence; tomorrow they get a single
auto-reply:

> "Your submission didn't fit this quarter's roster. Here's the path most
> of our signed artists took before they signed:
> the Neural Ghost Protocol™. → [link]"

Channel match scorecard:
- Audience already raised their hand (intent: high).
- Founder already operates this surface (no new platform to build).
- Conversion happens at the moment of disappointment, when the buyer is
  most willing to pay to close the gap.

Secondary channel (no extra build cost): the existing IG/X account posts
30-second stem walkthroughs that double as kit ads — each reel ends with
`gumroad.com/ocdrecords/neural-ghost`.

**Do not start a podcast, newsletter from zero, or TikTok account for
this launch.** They violate the no-new-channel rule.

   audience-building work.
