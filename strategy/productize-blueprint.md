## Your Core Transformation

**I help obsessed bedroom producers go from *stuck in the DAW with a folder
of unfinished loops* to *shipping a label-grade record with a signature
sonic signature* using the Entropy-to-Artifact Method™.**

Why this and not something broader (mixing tips, "how to get signed," etc.):
it's the one promise the OCD brand voice already makes — obsession becomes
artifact — and it's the exact gap 90%+ of the demos you reject fall into.
The transformation is specific, emotionally charged, and measurable
(finished master vs. unfinished project folder).

## Winning Product Structure

### Name
**The OCD Playbook: The Entropy-to-Artifact Method™**

The named mechanism is the four-stage protocol you already hint at in the
site copy — Entropy → Compression → Mastering → Artifact. Trademark the
phrase visually (not legally — just lock the name to OCD Records so nobody
else can use it without being obviously derivative).

### Contents (what's inside the ZIP)
1. **The Core PDF (≈60 pages)** — the full Entropy-to-Artifact Method:
   each stage has a decision tree, an exit criterion ("you leave Entropy
   when X is true"), and a failure mode catalog (why producers get stuck
   at each stage).
2. **DAW Template Pack** — pre-wired signal chains and arrangement
   skeletons for Ableton, FL, and Logic, named after the four stages so
   the method is embedded in the tool itself.
3. **The Compression Checklist** — 47 yes/no decisions a producer must
   answer before export. This is the piece people will screenshot and
   share; design it to be the viral artifact.
4. **The Reference Vault** — 25 annotated label-grade references across
   five genres, each marked up with *what* makes it releasable (not just
   "it sounds good").
5. **The Release Protocol** — a 30-day shippable calendar from final mix
   → master → artwork → DSP delivery → launch day.
6. **Transmission access** — the existing email list becomes the
   post-purchase channel: one broadcast per OCD release unpacking how
   the signed artist used the method on that specific record.

### Delivery (how it reaches buyers without you)
- Stripe Checkout button on a new `/playbook` page on ocdrecords.com.
- On purchase: Zapier/Make webhook → delivers the ZIP + grants access to
  a password-gated `/playbook/vault` page (served by the same static site
  — no new infra).
- Buyer is auto-subscribed to Transmission and tagged `playbook-buyer` so
  future upsells (Vault, Engine) target them specifically.
- 100% async, 100% self-serve. Zero founder presence required to deliver.

### Price point
**$97 one-time**, with a $297 "+ demo teardown" upsell (async video —
recorded, not live) that converts ~10–15% of buyers.

Rationale: $97 is the impulse-buy ceiling for an inspired producer at
2 a.m., sits below Mix with the Masters / Pensado's Place reference
pricing, and still clears ~$90 margin per unit on digital-only costs.
The $297 async upsell extracts willingness-to-pay from the top decile
without turning you into a consultant.

### What makes it irreplaceable
Not the PDF — anyone can write a PDF. The moat is **proof**: every
record you release under OCD is a case study for the Playbook, and
every Playbook buyer becomes a warm submitter to the label. The
methodology and the label co-sign each other. A competitor can copy
the frame; they cannot copy the catalog.

## Distribution Channel (match existing platform — no cold start)

**The `/submit` page.** You already receive demo submissions through
ocdrecords.com/submit, and by the nature of A&R you reject the large
majority. Today that rejection is dead weight — a one-line "not for us."

Rewire it: every rejection fires an automated email — *"You were close.
Here's the exact gap, and the method we use to close it with signed
artists. →   The OCD Playbook."* Submitters are pre-qualified (they
already want what OCD represents), emotionally primed (they just got
told no), and arrive on the sales page already believing the method
works — the catalog proves it.

Secondary amplification uses channels you already run, with zero new
platforms required: clip the Compression Checklist as a carousel on IG,
drop a 90-second "why this mix got signed" teardown on YouTube Shorts
once per release, and pin the Playbook link in the SoundCloud profile.
Transmission (the existing list) becomes the retention loop.
