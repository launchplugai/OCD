## Winning Product Structure

### Name
**The Entropy Vault** — "Stems from the signal chain. Licensed for your artifact."

Positioned as the **artifact layer** of the Neural × Human Signal Chain™ — the
raw material the label uses internally, now opened to outside producers and
sync houses at a controlled tier.

### Contents (launch catalog)
Pulled from assets the label already owns:

- **40 drum one-shots** + **20 texture beds** rendered from _Entropy 001_
  and the Neural Ghost back-catalog (24-bit / 48 kHz WAV).
- **12 full stem packs** (kick / bass / lead / atmos / FX / vox) from
  released tracks — the same stems normally reserved for the Enterprise
  licensing tier.
- **A "Decay Pack"** — 30 field recordings and granular textures captured
  during sessions (rain on the Berlin console room windows, tape artifacts,
  modular self-oscillation drift). These cannot be reproduced; that is the
  moat.
- **Signal Chain reference PDF** — one-page diagram of how each stem was
  processed, naming the console (SSL 4000G+), the chain (1176LN → LA-2A →
  Pultec), and the neural pass. Education _is_ product.
- **Clearance scroll** — a plain-English license grant covering commercial
  use up to 500K impressions, matching the Tier 01 Standard License already
  on the site. No lawyers required for the buyer.

### Delivery mechanism (works at 3am without you)
- Stripe Checkout → fulfillment via **SendOwl** (or Gumroad, if faster).
- Automated email with signed download links + the one-page clearance scroll.
- Single-page vault area at **`/vault`** on `ocdrecords.com`, gated behind a
  magic-link login (Memberstack or Outseta, no-code).
- Zero human touch from purchase → download → license grant.
- Monthly drop email ("New transmission") on the same ESP already powering the
  site footer's "Join Transmission" form.

### What makes it irreplaceable
1. **Provenance** — these are the actual stems from releases shown on the
   site, not sample-pack filler. Discoverable by any buyer who already
   streamed Neural Ghost.
2. **The Decay Pack field recordings** are literally non-reproducible.
3. **The Signal Chain reference PDF** teaches the method in parallel; every
   buyer walks away a little more fluent in the OCD aesthetic, which feeds
   the Tier 02/03 licensing pipeline and the studio booking funnel.
4. **Clearance is baked in.** 90% of competitor sample packs leave buyers
   unsure what they can commercially release. OCD's label-grade clearance
   scroll is a differentiator competitors cannot copy without a label.

### Price point
- **$79 one-time** — _Starter Vault_ (drum one-shots + textures + PDF).
- **$29/mo subscription** — _Transmission_ (full stem packs, monthly drops,
  Decay Pack access). Cancel anytime; license perpetual on anything downloaded.
- **$499 one-time** — _Signal Chain Archive_ (full back-catalog stems +
  all future drops for 12 months).

Rationale: anchored just above Splice/Loopcloud ($13/mo) but below boutique
packs like Goldbaby / Spitfire bundles ($100+). The $29 tier is the volume
engine; the $499 archive is the whale offer. Target blended margin ~92%.

## Week 1 Build Roadmap

Three tasks, roughly 10 hours total — do them in this order, do not skip
step 1.

1. **Carve the Starter Vault (4 hrs).** Pull stems from the Entropy 001
   session files already sitting on the studio drive. Render 40 drum
   one-shots and 20 texture beds to 24-bit / 48 kHz WAV. Build one
   consolidated .zip. Tag every file with the originating release.

2. **Stand up `/vault` with Stripe + SendOwl (4 hrs).** Clone the look of
   `licensing.html` so the vault reads as a native label surface, not a
   store bolted on. Three price buttons (Starter / Transmission / Archive),
   Stripe Checkout, SendOwl fulfillment, a one-page "What is the Vault?"
   section above the pricing. Draft the clearance scroll — it can mirror
   the Tier 01 Standard License language already on `licensing.html`.

3. **Shoot one launch Reel (2 hrs).** Camera on the SSL 4000G+ console.
   Pull up one stem, run it through the chain, show the waveform transform,
   caption: "This stem is in the Vault. $79." Post on Neural Ghost +
   label IG the same hour `/vault` goes live. Send the "Join Transmission"
   list a single launch email with the same clip.
