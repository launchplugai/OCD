## Your Core Transformation

**I help obsessed bedroom producers of cinematic / high-fidelity electronic music go from "unreleasable demo folder" to "shippable, licensable, catalog-grade release" using the Neural Ritual™ — the same 5-stage A&R protocol OCD Records uses to accept or reject every submission.**

The five stages of the Neural Ritual™ (this is the proprietary framework — reuse the same names across the product, the site, and the emails):

1. **Signal** — the A&R filter. What actually makes a track pass OCD's inbox.
2. **Sculpt** — production-level sound design against the OCD reference stems.
3. **Structure** — arrangement + mastering-chain that survives licensing QC.
4. **Sigil** — visual identity for the release (artwork, catalog #, metadata).
5. **Ship** — release cadence + licensing pipeline hand-off.

## Winning Product Structure

- **Name:** **The Obsession Vault** — powered by the Neural Ritual™
- **Contents:**
  - The **Neural Ritual™ Playbook** (~60pp PDF + web-reader) covering the 5 stages above
  - **40 reference stems** from OCD's catalog (contract-cleared, license-restricted usage — irreplaceable outside the label)
  - **Mastering-chain preset packs** for Ableton, Logic, FL, Reaper
  - The **A&R Filter Checklist** — the actual criteria used to accept or reject a submission on `submit.html` (no generic YouTube video can copy this, because the filter belongs to the label)
  - Bonus: **3 recorded studio walkthroughs** of shipped OCD releases with voice-over commentary — evidence, not theory
- **Delivery mechanism (fully async):**
  - Stripe checkout → Gumroad-hosted zip + gated web-membership page
  - Beehiiv (or Mailchimp) sequence: welcome → stage 1 → stage 2 → … → license-tier upsell
  - Zero human touch after checkout. If a customer needs support, an autoresponder points to the playbook chapter.
- **Price point:** **$67** (anchored below a course, above an ebook — above impulse for a serious hobbyist, below the "I need to think about this" cliff). Optional **$147 tier** adds one **queued** demo-review credit that runs on the next open submission cycle — still not-live, still async, still fails-gracefully if the queue is long.
- **What makes it irreplaceable:**
  - The **reference stems** and the **A&R Filter Checklist** are only obtainable through the label. Every generic "how to master electronic music" course competes on tutorials; this competes on **proprietary artifacts a competitor structurally cannot copy**.
  - The Neural Ritual™ name gives it a spine customers can quote. "I ran my track through the Neural Ritual" is the kind of sentence that spreads.

## Distribution Channel That Matches the Existing Platform

**The rejection funnel on `submit.html`.**

The label's own submission page is already receiving the highest-intent traffic on the internet for this product — artists who self-identify as obsessed and specifically want to be on OCD. Today, rejections presumably end in silence (or a form-letter).

Change: every rejected demo receives an autoresponder along the lines of:

> Your track didn't clear this cycle. Here's the A&R filter we used, and the Vault that walks you through the Ritual we would have applied.

This turns the label's highest-intent traffic into paid customers with **zero new channel to start from zero on**. Secondary distribution: the "Join Transmission" newsletter that's already in every page footer — one launch drop, one weekly Neural Ritual excerpt for four weeks after.

**Do not start a TikTok, YouTube channel, or paid ad experiment for launch.** The submission funnel + the transmission newsletter are the current-platform channels the rules require.

## Week 1 Build Roadmap

Three tasks that can start immediately and unblock everything downstream:

1. **Write the A&R Filter Checklist as a single-page PDF.** No new IP — write down the criteria already being applied to submissions today. This is the wedge asset, the lead magnet, and the spine of the Vault's opening chapter.
2. **Wire the rejection autoresponder on `submit.html`** through the existing form pipeline into Beehiiv/Mailchimp, delivering the checklist as the first email and the Vault as the second. Stripe checkout can be a Gumroad link on day 1; upgrade to a self-hosted checkout later.
3. **Record one studio walkthrough** (~30 min) of a shipped OCD release. This becomes both the "proof" module inside the Vault and the launch video for the Transmission newsletter drop. One recording session, permanent asset.

Ship in that order. If only #1 gets done this week, the funnel still improves — every subsequent piece stacks on top of it.
