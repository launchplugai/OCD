## Winning Product: **The Obsession Codex™**

**What it is:** a self-contained digital release system that walks an obsessive producer from finished demo to released + sync-registered master without ever touching your calendar.

### Contents (what's inside)

1. **The Protocol Playbook (PDF, ~80 pages)** — the full 7-stage framework, each stage with a decision tree, a worked example from an OCD Records release, and a "fail state" checklist.
2. **The Metadata Vault Template** — a prefilled spreadsheet covering ISRCs, split sheets, PRO registration, Sound Recording / Composition metadata, and sync-ready stem naming conventions.
3. **The Delivery Spec Pack** — DDP/WAV/MP3 export presets, loudness targets per destination (Spotify/Apple/sync libraries/vinyl), and a QC checklist.
4. **Contract Vault** — attorney-reviewable templates: single-song deal, split sheet, sync license, master use license, producer agreement.
5. **The Pitch Codex** — 20 annotated swipe-file examples of pitch emails that have actually gotten plays: sync supervisors, blog editors, playlist curators. Names redacted; subject lines, framing, and attachments shown.
6. **The Release Runway Calendar** — a 90-day Notion board the buyer duplicates: every task, every asset, every deadline, prewired to the Protocol.
7. **Bonus — Diagnostic Audio Lesson** — one evergreen 30-min video: "How to listen to your demo like an A&R." Recorded once, delivered forever.

### What makes it irreplaceable

- **Named, codified framework.** The OBSESSION PROTOCOL™ is yours; nobody else is selling it. Copying the content without the framework is incoherent; copying the framework without your catalog case studies is unconvincing.
- **Receipts, not theory.** Every stage anchors to a real OCD Records release (Entropy 001, etc.) with before/after audio and metadata. Competitors sell theory; you sell *provenance*.
- **The Metadata Vault + Delivery Spec Pack are mechanically hard to fake.** They encode years of label-side scar tissue (PRO registration quirks, sync library naming conventions, loudness delivery per platform). A generic course cannot ship these.

### Delivery mechanism (works at 3am)

- Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy → auto-delivers the Codex ZIP + Notion board duplication link.
- Payment triggers a 7-email onboarding sequence (ConvertKit / Beehiiv), one per Protocol stage.
- The Notion board is the "live" product surface — update it quarterly, buyers get the new version automatically. No calls, no DMs, no live presence required.
- Hosted on a new subdomain: `codex.ocd-records.com` → links from `submit.html` and the catalog footer.

**$297 one-time, with a $47/mo "Vault Access" upsell** for continuous updates + new swipe files + the private release-feed Slack/Discord.

Rationale: $297 is above the "ebook" perception bracket (where anything <$100 gets treated as a leadmagnet), below the "I need to think about this for a week" bracket (>$500). It matches the willingness-to-pay of a producer who already owns $2K in plugins and a $1,500 interface — the exact buyer. The $47/mo recurring converts the one-time hit into catalog revenue that compounds.
