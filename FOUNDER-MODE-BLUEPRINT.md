## Winning Product

**Format A wins on margin + the distribution fit** (the `/submit` page is already the funnel). Format B ships as the upsell inside every Report.

### Name
**The Obsession Index™ Report** — powered by the SIGNAL-to-ARTIFACT Protocol.

### Contents (every buyer receives)

1. **The Obsession Index Score** (0–100) — composite across the 7 SIGNAL dimensions, benchmarked against the OCD catalog.
2. **Dimension Breakdown** — scored panels for: _Sound design originality, Intuition (emotional arc), Grind (mix tightness), Novelty (sonic fingerprint), Attack (opening 15s hook), Legacy (10-year listen test), Artifact (masters-grade loudness/dynamic range)_.
3. **Sync-Licensing Fit Heatmap** — which of 8 placement categories (trailer, prestige TV, video game, sports promo, tech ad, indie film, luxury brand, true-crime doc) the track best fits, with 3 named reference cues per category.
4. **The One Fix** — the single highest-leverage change (named, timestamped) that would move the score the most.
5. **Resubmission Pass** — one free rescore within 30 days (locks the buyer into the loop).
6. **Signed-Artist Fast-Track** — scores ≥85 get a direct A&R review by OCD, bypassing the open submission queue.

### Delivery (how it reaches the buyer with you asleep)

- **Front door:** existing `submit.html` → new "Get your Obsession Index Report — $49" button alongside the free submission option.
- **Pipeline:** Stripe Checkout → webhook fires → worker pulls audio → runs a scoring pass (any current audio-analysis API + a small rubric prompt against OCD's reference catalog) → renders the PDF from a template → emails the buyer. No human in the loop.
- **Upsell inside the PDF:** the final page links to the **$97 SIGNAL-to-ARTIFACT Blueprint** (Format B) with a time-boxed discount. That's your recurring margin stack.

### Price point & rationale

- **$49** entry (Report). Low enough that an unsigned artist spends it on impulse after a "label rejected me" moment; high enough that one Stripe fee leaves ~$45 net at ~92% margin. Bundled resubmission makes it feel like $49 for two reads.
- **$97** upsell (Blueprint). Priced below the pain threshold where buyers ask for a refund, above the price where they treat it as disposable.
- **Expected AOV** once the upsell attaches at a conservative 15%: ~$63.

### What makes it irreplaceable

- The score is **benchmarked against the OCD catalog**, not a generic rubric. No competitor can replicate that without signing the same artists.
- The fast-track lane turns the Report into the only paid A&R assessment in the industry that can _actually end in a signing_. That's the moat.
- The named framework (SIGNAL-to-ARTIFACT) is yours — every report trains the market to recognize it.
