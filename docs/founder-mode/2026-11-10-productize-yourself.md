## Winning Product Structure

### Name
**OCD ENTROPY PACK — The Sync-Ready Operating Kit for Underground Producers**
Proprietary mechanism: **The ENTROPY Method™** (Extract · Normalize · Tag · Register · Pitch Once/Pitch Forever).

### Contents
1. **ENTROPY Method Playbook** (40-page PDF) — the 5-step system, with annotated examples from OCD catalog releases.
2. **Sync Metadata Template Pack** — CSV + XLSX templates with Google-DSP-aligned fields (mood, tempo, instrumentation, clearance flags, one-stop confirmation).
3. **Loudness & Delivery Presets** — iZotope Insight, Ableton, Logic, and Pro Tools presets hitting −14 LUFS integrated / −1 dBTP for streaming + sync.
4. **Legal Mini-Kit** — fill-in-the-blank split sheet, LOI, one-stop confirmation, and work-for-hire waiver (reviewed by IP counsel; jurisdiction notes included).
5. **Pitch Scripts & Sync Brief Decoder** — 12 proven cold-pitch templates to music supervisors, plus a decoder that converts a brief ("gritty, distorted, 90 BPM, cinematic") into which catalog tracks to send.
6. **ENTROPY Walkthrough Videos** (4 × 15 min, async, Loom) — unlisted, hosted on the OCD domain behind a Gumroad license key.
7. **Lifetime Updates Clause** — buyers get every revision; creates an evergreen upsell list.

### What Makes It Irreplaceable
- **Catalog-proven** — every template is one the label actually uses on live releases (ENTROPY-001, etc.).
- **Named framework** — "The ENTROPY Method" becomes a search term over time; the label's own releases are the proof.
- **Legal kit** — most producer courses don't touch paperwork because they aren't labels. This is OCD's moat.
- **Updates ratchet** — each new OCD release adds a case-study appendix, so the product compounds in value without new work.

### Delivery (works at 3am)
- Hosted on **Gumroad** (or Lemon Squeezy) linked from the existing OCD Records site nav.
- Gumroad handles payments, license keys, email delivery, VAT, refunds, upsells.
- Delivery email is automated; videos stream from unlisted Vimeo with domain-lock.
- No live component. Buyer completes checkout → gets the pack → never meets the founder.

### Price Point
**$147 one-time**, with a $47 "Lite" cut-down (templates only) and a $497 "Catalog Submission Fast-Track" tier that bundles a priority OCD submissions review (automated queue flag, not a 1:1 call — stays leverage-safe).

Rationale: $147 sits below the "needs manager approval" threshold for indie producers, well above the $27 Gumroad-template graveyard that signals low value. Three-tier structure captures price-elastic buyers without introducing a human service.
