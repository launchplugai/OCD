## Winning Product Structure

### Name
**OCD SYNC PROTOCOL™** — *The 5-Layer Signature Method for Sync-Ready Electronic & Cinematic Music*

The proprietary mechanism is the **5-Layer Signature**: Focus · Restraint · Emotional Arc · Quantized Endings · Negative Space. Every accepted OCD Records release maps to these five; every rejected demo misses at least two.

### Contents
1. **The 5-Layer Signature framework** — 5 core video modules (~15 min each) with the annotated reject-vs-accept audio pairs from OCD's own submission pile.
2. **The Stem & Metadata Kit** — Ableton / Logic / FL templates for stem-splitting, plus a Notion database pre-wired for the exact metadata fields music supervisors filter on (BPM, key, mood cluster, ISRC, one-stop clearance flag).
3. **The Supervisor's Ear listening library** — 30 licensed cues with a supervisor's-eye teardown of *why* each got placed.
4. **Cue-Sheet & Split-Sheet Generator** — an Airtable template that outputs a filed-ready cue sheet the moment a track is delivered.
5. **The OCD Fast Lane** — top-scoring student submissions skip the public queue and go straight to A&R review for the licensing catalog. This is the irreplaceable moat: no other course can offer a real label's submission bypass.

### Delivery (works at 3am)

- Stripe Payment Link on the OCD site → automated Zapier flow issues Vimeo access + Airtable duplicate + Notion invite.
- 7-day onboarding email sequence via ConvertKit / Loops.
- The "Fast Lane" runs on your existing `submit.html` — a gated form field asks for a Sync Protocol receipt ID; validated submissions land in a separate A&R queue.

### Price Point
- **$497 one-time** OR **$57/mo × 12**.
- Rationale: one library sync placement typically earns $500–$5,000. The product pays for itself with a single hit. High enough to filter for serious producers (raises completion + testimonials); low enough to sell without a call.
