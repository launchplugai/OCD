## Winning Product Structure

**Name:** *The Sonic Architecture Codex — The Neural Pass Method™*

**Contents (the proprietary mechanism is the spine, not the volume):**

1. **80-page Codex PDF** — the philosophy + the four-layer Neural Pass Method
   walked through screenshot-by-screenshot.
2. **Reference Stems Pack** — three real OCD Records masters (one per genre lane:
   Neural Electronic, Cinematic/Orchestral, Dark Techno) delivered as raw stems
   + the final printed master, so buyers can A/B their own mixes against label output.
3. **DAW Session Templates** — fully wired Ableton Live and FL Studio projects
   demonstrating each pass, so the methodology is executable, not just readable.
4. **Plugin Chain Sheets** — printable PDFs of the exact signal chains the roster
   uses, with settings and intent annotations.
5. **The Auto-Reject Checklist** — the same internal filter OCD's A&R applies to
   inbound demos, turned into a 14-point self-audit before buyers hit submit.
6. **Submission Template Pack** — pre-formatted demo email + Bandcamp/Soundcloud
   listing copy that maps 1:1 to the fields on `submit.html`.

**What makes it irreplaceable:** competitors sell sample packs or generic mixing
courses. This is the *only* product that comes with **real masters from a working
label** as reference material plus the **same A&R rubric** the label uses on inbound
demos. The Neural Pass Method™ name protects it as a defensible methodology, not a
swap-out tutorial.

**Delivery (zero live presence):**
- Hosted on **Gumroad** (Stan Store as backup) — instant ZIP download on Stripe
  payment.
- Automated email sequence via ConvertKit: receipt → unzip guide → day-3 "first pass"
  prompt → day-14 "submit your demo" CTA.
- Buyer-only Discord role auto-granted via Zapier so support is asynchronous.
- All assets pre-rendered. No live calls, no per-customer customization.

**Price point:** **$97 launch (first 100 buyers) → $147 standard.**
Rationale: above the impulse-buy threshold so it filters tire-kickers, below the
$200 ceiling that triggers credit-card-partner-check hesitation for the target
(working producers 22–35). Perceived value of the stems pack alone is ~$400 in
Splice-equivalent licensing.

## Distribution Channel (matches your existing platform)

**`submit.html` is the channel.** Every artist who fills out the OCD submission form
is, by definition, the exact buyer persona — obsessive, ambitious, willing to be
judged by the label. After form submit, redirect to a thank-you page that reads:
*"We review every demo within 14 business days. While you wait, study the same
playbook our roster uses →"* with a link to the Codex.

This costs nothing, ships with existing traffic, and converts at the moment of
maximum intent. Secondary amplification: a 30-second Instagram reel per chapter of
the Codex on `@ocdrecords`, since the brand and visual language already exist.

## Week 1 Build Roadmap

1. **Lock the methodology.** Draft a one-page diagram of the Neural Pass Method
   (Skeleton → Tension → Resonance → Master), then expand it into the Codex outline
   with one chapter per pass. *(~8 hours)*
2. **Package the proof.** Export three released OCD masters as labelled stems, save
   the source Ableton + FL projects, and screenshot the plugin chains into the
   Signal Sheet PDFs. *(~10 hours)*
3. **Ship the storefront and the funnel.** Stand up the Gumroad listing (copy,
   mockups, price), wire Stripe + ConvertKit + Discord automation, and add the
   post-submit redirect on `submit.html` pointing at the Codex landing block.
   *(~4 hours)*

Total Week 1: ~22 hours of focused build, leaving headroom in a 10-hr/week budget
across two weeks before launch.
