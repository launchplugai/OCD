## Winning Product Structure

### Name
**The Neural Precision Protocol™ — Sonic Architecture Vault**

The proprietary mechanism is the **Neural Precision Protocol™ (NPP)** — a
named 5-stage methodology that maps every step from idea to label-ready
submission. The Vault is the toolkit that operationalizes NPP without the
founder in the room.

The 5 stages of NPP:
1. **Frequency Selection** — picking a sonic palette the catalog actually slots into
2. **Sonic Architecture** — arrangement skeleton derived from OCD's released catalog
3. **Neural Precision Mix** — the OCD mix-bus chain + reference-track translation rubric
4. **Human Intuition Pass** — the curated "does this belong on OCD" checklist
5. **Submission Protocol** — the metadata + packet format A&R will actually open

### Contents
- **Project templates** for Ableton Live 12, Logic Pro, FL Studio (3 templates × 3 DAWs = 9 starting points, tied to OCD's catalog sub-genres)
- **OCD Mix-Bus Chain** — the exact serial of plug-ins/settings used on released catalog masters, exported as DAW presets + a PDF rationale
- **The Obsession Sample Pack** — 400 royalty-free one-shots and 80 loops, tagged with BPM/key, drawn from OCD's archive
- **NPP Workbook (PDF, 40 pages)** — the protocol written down, fillable
- **Submission Packet Template** — the exact metadata/format the OCD submit.html page is configured to score highest on
- **5 video walkthroughs** (15–25 min each) — recorded once, hosted on a private Cloudflare Stream URL
- **The Label-Readiness Self-Audit** — a single-page scorecard that mirrors the A&R rubric

### What Makes It Irreplaceable
- The Vault is keyed to **this label's actual catalog and submissions pipeline**. Any producer who runs it produces work that fits OCD's brief by construction.
- Buyers get **submission priority**: a coupon code in the Vault routes their next submit.html upload to the top of the A&R queue. This is the moat — no competitor can offer a real release shot.
- The proprietary name (NPP) and stage labels become the vocabulary of the buyer's own progress — they can't describe what they learned without naming the protocol.

### Delivery Mechanism (zero-presence)
- Checkout: **Lemon Squeezy** (or Gumroad) embedded on a new `vault.html` page in this repo. Handles tax/VAT, license keys, and instant delivery.
- Asset delivery: **Cloudflare R2** signed URLs in the receipt email — no manual handoff.
- Email drip: **Resend + a static 7-email sequence** drips one NPP stage per day, ending with the priority-submission coupon.
- Support: **a single Notion FAQ page** linked from the receipt. No live calls.
- Submission integration: the existing `submit.html` form accepts a Vault key field, which queues the submission into a priority tag in whatever backend handles A&R.

### Price Point
**$97 one-time** for the Vault, with a **$47 launch price for the first 100
buyers** (creates the urgency the OCD aesthetic already trades on).

Rationale: producers routinely pay $200–500 for a single sample pack from
Splice top sellers, and $50–150 for a Serum preset pack. $97 sits below
sample-pack price ceiling but well above impulse-buy resistance — buyers who
pay $97 self-select as serious. Margin at $97 with ~$2 in payment fees and $0
marginal delivery cost is **~98%**. A $19/mo upsell tier (FrequencyOS audit
when shipped) follows in Phase 2.

**The `submit.html` page is already the distribution channel.** Producers

> "Submission Received. Want to know why most demos here are passed on — and

on existing organic traffic** — no new platform, no new audience, no cold
acquisition.

Secondary surfaces that already exist in this repo, ranked:

No new channel. No starting from zero.

## Week 1 Build Roadmap — Start Immediately

1. **Day 1–2: Lock the NPP framework on paper.** Write the 5 stages as a
   one-page spec. Pull 5 released OCD tracks; reverse-engineer the
   arrangement, mix-bus, and metadata patterns. This becomes the spine the
   Vault contents are organized around. No coding yet.

2. **Day 3–5: Build the Label-Readiness Self-Audit page.** Add `audit.html`
   to this repo — a single-page scorecard (9 questions, instant score, 2
   dimensions explained free, the other 7 gated behind email + Vault offer).
   Wire it into `submit.html` as the post-submission tripwire. This ships the
   funnel before the product exists, captures the waitlist, and validates
   demand.

3. **Day 6–7: Set up the storefront skeleton.** Create the Lemon Squeezy (or
   Gumroad) product entry at the $47 launch price, add `vault.html` to this
   repo with the embedded checkout, draft the 7-email Resend drip stubs.
   Storefront stays in "Launching [date]" mode while assets are produced in
   weeks 2–4.

## What's Explicitly Rejected

Per the rules:

- **Live coaching, 1:1 mentorship, Discord office hours** — fails the 3am
  test, requires founder presence.
- **Generic "How to make electronic music" course** — no proprietary
  framework, no moat.
- **Starting a YouTube channel / TikTok for launch traffic** — violates the
  distribution rule (current platform is the OCD site, not a personal
  creator account).
- **A&R review service sold as the product** — the review is the *bonus that
  funnels back into label discovery*, not the deliverable. Selling reviews
  would cap revenue at founder hours.
