## Your Core Transformation

**I help AI-native bedroom producers go from *unsigned, uncurated, unlicensable*
to *release-ready, catalog-grade, sync-eligible* — using the OCD Curation
Protocol™: a 7-gate quality standard that fuses human intuition with neural
precision.**

The 7 gates (named so it's ownable, not a generic checklist):

1. **Signal** — is there a physical artifact hiding in the track?
2. **Intent** — one sentence the artist can defend under pressure.
3. **Frequency** — spectral / loudness discipline against reference targets.
4. **Entropy** — the calibrated amount of chaos that keeps it human.
5. **Identity** — visual + sonic + naming lock (Neural Ghost / Entropy 001 style).
6. **Rights** — stems, splits, sample clearance, sync-ready metadata.
7. **Release Physics** — timing, drop cadence, transmission plan.

## Winning Product Structure

- **Name:** **OCD Curation Protocol™ — the 7-Gate Release Standard for AI-Native
  Producers**

- **Contents:**
  1. **The Protocol Manual (PDF, ~50 pp)** — one chapter per gate, with the
     *why*, the pass/fail criteria, and the OCD Records house standard.
  2. **7 Gate Scorecards (fillable PDF + Notion)** — the same rubric you use
     internally to greenlight a release.
  3. **The Reference Vault** — 20 curated reference tracks per gate
     (playlists, not files — legally clean) with annotated notes on *why*.
  4. **The Entropy Presets Pack** — a small stems / MIDI / plugin-preset
     bundle that demonstrates gate #4 (Entropy) in practice.
  5. **The Transmission Plan Template (Notion)** — the release-physics
     calendar OCD uses: drop cadence, teaser rhythm, licensing outreach.
  6. **The Submission Fast-Lane Token** — every buyer gets one guaranteed
     human review of a track against the protocol via `submit.html` (async,
     video verdict — recorded, not live; batched weekly).
  7. **Living updates** — quarterly protocol revisions emailed to buyers so
     the asset appreciates over time.

- **Delivery mechanism (no live presence required):**
  - Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy checkout linked from `index.html` and
    `submit.html`.
  - Instant email with signed download links (S3 / R2 + expiring URL).
  - Notion template duplicated via public share link.
  - Fast-lane review handled async in weekly batches — you record a 3-minute
    video verdict per track, so buyers get a human decision without you being
    online. Cap the tokens if volume ever exceeds your batch window.

- **What makes it irreplaceable:**
  - It's the **actual internal standard** OCD Records uses to greenlight a
    release — not a course written to sell a course.
  - The **fast-lane token** is a real signal path into a real label, not a
    promise of one. That is the moat.
  - Named, numbered, defensible **7 gates** — competitors can copy language
    but not the reference vault or the label attached to it.

- **Price point:** **$149 launch → $249 within 60 days.**
  - $149 anchors it as a serious tool, not a $27 impulse PDF, and matches the
    OCD Records tone (obsession, precision).
  - $249 within 60 days once the first 25–50 buyers produce testimonials and
    at least one signed release traced back to the protocol.
  - Later: **$497 "Studio tier"** bundling three fast-lane tokens + a stem
    review — same product, no live time from you.

## Distribution Channel — Match, Don't Start From Zero

**Channel: your own site + the "Join Transmission" newsletter.**

You already own the surface: `index.html`, `submit.html`, `studios.html`,
`licensing.html`, and a newsletter capture ("Join Transmission") in the
footer of every page. Every producer who lands on `submit.html` and hesitates
is your ideal buyer for the protocol — they want to be signed, but their
track can't clear the gates yet. Add a soft CTA on `submit.html`:

> "Not sure your track clears the 7 gates? Run it through the OCD
> Curation Protocol™ before you submit."

Secondary lane, only after Transmission is warm: post one gate per week as
short-form on the `launchplugai` handle (X / IG / TikTok reels) — extracts
from the manual, not new content — driving back to the product page. This is
distribution *from existing surface*, not a new channel to build.

Do **not** start a YouTube, a podcast, or a TikTok from zero for this launch.
