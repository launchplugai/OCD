## Winning Product Structure

- **Name:** **The Obsessive Delivery Kit™** — powered by the *Curator Score* framework
- **Contents:**
  1. **The Curator Score rubric** — 12-point weighted self-A&R tool (technical readiness + commercial fit + brand alignment) with pass / warn / fail thresholds. *This is the proprietary mechanism.*
  2. **Master Delivery Checklist** — LUFS, true-peak, dynamic range, stems, metadata, cover-art specs, ISRC prep. PDF + Notion template.
  3. **Licensing & Split Pack** — 6 templates: bio, one-sheet, sync-brief response, master license, split sheet, exclusive vs non-exclusive.
  4. **The OCD Submission Vault** — vetted directory of 40+ labels and sync houses, filtered by genre / tier / current call status. Refreshed quarterly.
  5. **3 Annotated Teardowns** — real submissions with signed vs rejected verdicts and why, keyed to the Curator Score.
  6. **Bonus:** reference-mix waveform library with annotations.
- **Delivery:** Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy → automated Notion / Google Drive access on purchase → 5-email onboarding drip → optional buyer-only Discord role. Zero live touch after listing.
- **Price point:** **$127 launch (later $197).** Sits above the impulse tier — signals seriousness and funds paid ads — but well under the $500+ threshold that stalls solo producers already buying $50 plugins.

**Why it's irreplaceable:** the Curator Score is the only piece competitors can't copy without publishing their own rubric. Once it becomes the vocabulary producers use to talk about "is my track ready" — a "7/12 Curator Score" — the Kit owns the category.

## Distribution Channel (matches existing platform)

**The `ocdrecords` site's own `submit.html` funnel.** No new channel required.

- Every producer who lands on Submit is already self-identified as an "obsessed creator" — the exact ICP.
- Add a two-branch reply to submissions: if the demo passes the internal Curator Score, invite to label conversation; if it doesn't, route to a **soft-sell landing block** offering the Kit as the pre-qualification path ("here's the exact rubric we scored you on — level up and re-submit").
- Turns the label's rejection pile — which today generates zero revenue and zero goodwill — into a warm, self-qualifying buyer segment that funds the label.

Secondary channel: existing social handles wired into `index.html`'s footer (IG / X / YouTube / SoundCloud). Post one Curator-Score teardown per week — proof-of-work content that doubles as top-of-funnel.

## Week 1 Build Roadmap

1. **Write the Curator Score rubric v1.** 12 weighted criteria across technical / commercial / brand-fit, with pass / warn / fail thresholds and worked examples. Half a day. This is the moat — do it first.
2. **Ship the four core assets.** Master Delivery Checklist, Curator Score worksheet (fillable PDF + Notion), the 6 licensing templates, and Submission Vault v1 (40 hand-picked labels/sync houses with contact + current-brief status). ~2 days.
3. **List and wire the funnel.** Publish the Gumroad/Lemon Squeezy listing, drop a Kit landing block into a new `kit.html` on the site, and update `submit.html` to route non-fit demos to it. Half a day.

Everything after Week 1 is content marketing (weekly teardowns) and monitoring conversion from `submit.html` → Kit purchase → re-submission. The Kit lives on its own from day one; nothing here requires you to be online at 3am.
