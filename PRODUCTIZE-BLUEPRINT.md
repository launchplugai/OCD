## Winning Product Structure

**Name:** OCD PrecisionKit™ — *The Sync-Ready Master Bundle*

**Proprietary mechanism inside:** the **Neural Precision Protocol™** (Signal → Sculpt → Master → Sync) — the same 4-stage pipeline OCD's licensing team audits every submission against.

**Contents:**
1. **40+ mastering-chain presets** — SSL, FabFilter Pro-Q/L, iZotope Ozone, tuned to the LUFS / true-peak / dynamic-range window sync agencies actually clear.
2. **5 DAW project templates** — Ableton Live, Logic Pro, FL Studio, Cubase, Reaper — pre-routed to the Protocol's 4 stages.

4. **The Sync-Ready Checklist™ (PDF)** — 27-point technical + legal spec sheet. Loudness targets by platform (Netflix -27 LKFS, YouTube -14, Spotify -14, cinema -23), stem-delivery format, metadata requirements, PRO registration, split-sheet template.
5. **Clearance Affidavit template** — the exact document OCD uses to green-light sync-ready tracks. Ready to sign with collaborators.
6. **Onboarding email drip** — 7-day sequence walking through Signal → Sculpt → Master → Sync with one video per stage.

**Delivery mechanism (0 live presence):**
`ocd-records.com/precisionkit` landing page → Stripe / Gumroad checkout → automated license key + .zip download + private Notion companion doc → Kit-Klavier email drip. Zero human touch after purchase. Support handled via a canned-response knowledge base for the first 90 days; if volume warrants, hand off to a $15/hr VA on a shared inbox.

**Price point:** **$147 Standard** / **$47 Lite (presets + checklist only)** / **$497 Pro (Standard + 3-month Circle community access + one roster teardown recording per month)**.
- Rationale: $147 sits below the $199 producer-plugin psychological threshold; $47 captures impulse tier and rejection-funnel traffic; $497 is the natural upsell to Format B once demand is proved. Break-even in weeks 3–4 at ~35 Standard units.

**What makes it irreplaceable:**
- Only mastering / clearance toolkit **audited by an active sync-licensing label with a live catalog and 24–48h A&R SLA.** Every other "sync bundle" on the market is authored by teachers, not by an entity with placements at stake.
