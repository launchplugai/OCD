## Winning Product Structure

### Name
**The Artifact Vault — powered by the Neural Precision Protocol™**

### Contents (what's inside)

1. **NPP Playbook (PDF, ~40 pages)** — the 7 stages OCD uses to take a sketch to catalog:
   1. *Signal Audit* (does the track have a non-negotiable idea?)
   2. *Frequency Map* (spectral fingerprint vs. catalog DNA)
   3. *Intuition Pass* (human-only listen, no meters)
   4. *Neural Pass* (AI critique against reference set)
   5. *Tension Calibration* (arrangement scoring rubric)
   6. *Artifact Finish* (mix/master gates — must hit before release)
   7. *Sync-Readiness Score* (license-clearance checklist)
2. **The OCD Stem Library** — 200+ royalty-free stems pulled from unreleased catalog sessions (Neural Ghost, Cobalt Void, Static Pulse, Lithium Soul), tagged by frequency map + tension role.
3. **NPP Reference Pack** — 12 anonymized A&R scorecards of real submissions (pass + fail), so buyers see the rubric applied.
4. **Preset Bank** — Serum / Vital / Ableton racks named by NPP stage (e.g. "Stage 4: Neural Pass — Cinematic Sub").
5. **Submission Fast-Pass** — one priority demo review slot per buyer per year, returned async via Loom (8–10 min, recorded, no live calls). Caps fulfillment cost and protects the "no presence" rule.

### Delivery (no presence required)
- Static download bundle hosted on **Gumroad** (or Lemon Squeezy) with delivery email.
- Customer-only landing page on `ocdrecords.com/vault` for updates + the Fast-Pass form.
- Loom reviews recorded in batches once per month, 90 minutes total — async, off-hours.

### What makes it irreplaceable
- The stems are **literally pulled from the OCD catalog** — nobody else can sell them.
- The NPP is **OCD's internal A&R rubric**, publicly named for the first time — it's the proprietary mechanism, not generic "tips."
- Every buyer's track gets one scorecard against the same rubric the roster passed — that loop is the moat.

### Price point
- **$197** single payment.
- Rationale: high enough to filter out hobbyists (Hormozi's "pay enough to pay attention"), low enough to skip a sales call. Sits cleanly under the $200 impulse threshold for producers who already spend $300+/yr on Splice/Plugin Boutique. Fast-Pass review costs OCD ~10 min/buyer/yr → margin stays above 90%.
- **Order bumps:** $47 *Stem Vol. 2* drop every 90 days; $97 *Artist Roster Deep-Dive* video bundle.

## Distribution Channel (matches what you already own)

**The Submission Funnel + Transmission Newsletter — re-pointed.**

You already run a high-intent inbound channel: producers actively submitting demos to OCD. Convert it to a two-step:

1. On `submit.html`, the *primary* path is still demo submission (free). The *secondary* path becomes: **"Want the rubric we'll grade you against? Get The Artifact Vault →"**. This is the highest-converting placement you have — the prospect is *already* asking for OCD's standard.
2. Every rejection / hold reply (the volume channel) ships with one Vault CTA in the signature. Rejections become revenue without a new audience.
3. *Transmission* newsletter does one feature per release cycle: "Inside Entropy 001 — the NPP scorecard." Soft Vault CTA at the bottom.

**Do not** start a TikTok, IG Reels, or YouTube channel for this. The roster's existing release posts plus your owned funnel are enough demand to validate $20k MRR before you ever need a new channel.
