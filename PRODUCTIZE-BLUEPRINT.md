## Your Core Transformation

**You help AI-augmented bedroom producers go from "experiments nobody licenses" to "sync-ready releases music supervisors actively pull from" — using the O2A (Obsession-to-Artifact) Protocol.**

The protocol, in six moves:

1. **Capture** — raw idea / AI generation / field recording
2. **Refine** — frequency surgery against the OCD reference curve
3. **Quantize** — translate intuition into the supervisor's brief language
4. **Master** — hybrid analog/digital chain to broadcast spec
5. **Tag** — metadata, BPM, mood, cue-sheet-ready
6. **Pitch** — submit through vetted, briefed channels

This is the proprietary mechanism. Every product packages a subset of it.

## Winning Product Structure

### Name
**The OCD Sync-Ready Vault™ — Powered by the O2A Protocol**

### Contents
1. **Reference Pack** — 10 OCD-mastered stems (one per genre lane: ambient, dark techno, cinematic, hauntologic, etc.), each annotated with the EQ / dynamics / saturation moves that took them from raw to placed
2. **Brief Decoder Library** — 60 entries translating supervisor-speak ("hauntological, sub-driven, 92 BPM, Mica Levi adjacent") into specific mix targets, BPM windows, and reference cues
3. **Signal-Chain Templates** — drop-in Ableton, Logic, and FL project files pre-loaded with the OCD chain (input gain staging → bus comp → tape sim → mastering chain)
4. **The O2A Checklist** — printable 1-page audit a producer runs on every track before submission
5. **Submission Ledger** — 200+ vetted sync agencies + libraries, current briefs, contact protocol, and lead-time expectations
6. **Quarterly Drop** — new briefs, new references, new agency intel; subscribers auto-receive

### Delivery
Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy → instant ZIP delivery → private Notion vault for the living docs (Brief Decoder + Submission Ledger). **No human in the loop, ever.** Refunds are auto-handled; quarterly drops are pre-recorded.

### Price Point
- **$297 one-time** (Founder's Tier — first 100 buyers, locks lifetime updates)
- **$197 + $19/mo** (Standard — get the vault + ongoing intel drops)

**Rationale:** Anchors against $200–500/track sync agency placement fees and $300–1500/track mastering. The vault is one rejected pitch in cost. Margin >90% after Stripe + storage.

### What Makes It Irreplaceable
The Brief Decoder and Submission Ledger are the moat — they encode the relational knowledge OCD already has from running the label + the licensing pipeline. A competitor can copy templates; they cannot copy the supervisor relationships and the language map.
