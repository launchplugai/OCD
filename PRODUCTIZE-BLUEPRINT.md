## Winning Product Structure

- **Name:** *The Obsession OS* — powered by the proprietary **Obsession Loop**
  framework (Curate → Persona → Release → License).
- **Contents:**
  1. The Obsession Loop framework (15-page PDF + Loom walkthrough).
  2. Notion workspace duplicate: catalog schema, release-cadence calendar,
     licensing-tier matrix, artist-roster CRM.
  3. Legal templates: sync agreement, master-use license, submission SLA,
     artist split sheet.
  4. AI-Persona prompt library: artist bio, cover-art generation system,
     press-kit boilerplate (Neural Ghost is the reference build).
  5. Fork-ready label website template — the current OCD Records site,
     extracted and rebranded in-app.
  6. Sync-licensing playbook: how to pitch music supervisors, tier pricing,
     a template outreach sequence.
- **Delivery:** Lemon Squeezy checkout → instant email with a Notion duplicate
  link, template zip, and Discord invite. Zero calls. Zero live handoff. A
  weekly async-only office-hour thread in Discord keeps community perceived
  value up without consuming time zones.
- **What makes it irreplaceable:** it is the *only* productized label OS whose
  proof-of-concept — Neural Ghost — publicly demonstrates the entire loop from
  AI persona to licensed catalog. The framework is not theory; the customer
  can click through the working case study before they buy.
- **Price point:** **$497 launch → $997 anchor within 60 days.** Anchor is
  justified by replacing at least three purchases the buyer would otherwise
  make separately: catalog site build ($2–5k), licensing legal templates
  ($500–1.5k), and a persona/branding sprint ($1–3k).

## Distribution Channel (matches existing platform)

The OCD Records website itself. It already ranks for the aesthetic and pulls
inbound submissions (`submit.html`). Add:

- `/method` landing page linking every existing catalog release back to a
  loop stage — visitors already browsing the catalog see the machinery that
  produced it.
- A one-line footer CTA on `artist.html` (Neural Ghost profile): *"This
  artist doesn't exist. This catalog earns anyway. See the method →"*
- Repurpose the existing submission-form traffic: applicants who don't fit
  the label get an auto-response offering the OS at a $100 first-buyer
  discount.

No new channel. No cold audience. The label *is* the funnel.
