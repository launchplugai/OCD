## Winning Product Structure

- **Name:** **The OCD Vault — The Frequency Architect's Toolkit**
  Proprietary mechanism: **OCD Frequency Architecture Method™** (the 7 stages:
  *Intent → Reference → Skeleton → Frequency Map → Headroom Audit → Sync
  Pre-Master → Submission Brief*).
- **Contents:**
  1. **Stem Library** — 500+ Neural-Ghost-spec stems pre-tagged by
     frequency slot (sub / low-mid / air).
  2. **Mix Bus Templates** — Ableton, Logic, FL Studio, presets locked to the
     OCD headroom + LUFS targets.
  3. **The Obsession Worksheet** — release/no-release decision tree, the same
     gate the label uses on incoming demos via `submit.html`.
  4. **Sync Submission Bible** — vetted music-supervisor contact list,
     pitch-email templates, brief format.
  5. **Frequency Architecture Method PDF** — the 7-stage protocol, with
     before/after spectrograms from the catalog.
  6. **Private Vault community** (gated by purchase) — async only, monthly
     "frequency teardown" video drop, *no live calls*.
- **Delivery:** Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy storefront → instant download +
  auto-enroll into the gated community. Zero seller presence required — the
  bot delivers everything; you ship the monthly teardown when you ship it.
- **Price point:** **$147 one-time** (anchor) with a **$297 "Architect"** tier
  that includes quarterly stem drops + supervisor list updates.
  - Rationale: sits above impulse-buy ($47 sample packs) and below
    "production course" ($497+). $147 is the supervisor's-coffee-budget
    price — high enough to signal craft, low enough that a producer with one
    placement covers it forever.
- **Why it's irreplaceable:** the stems and templates carry the *label's actual
  sonic signature* — a buyer can't reverse-engineer this from YouTube. The
  Submission Bible is contact-graph IP. The Method PDF anchors the brand vocabulary
  (now everyone in the community talks about "frequency slots" — that's
  category ownership).

  named-framework rule and dilutes the brand.

  match-your-current-platform rule; the submission funnel is the channel.
