// 02 The Sync Protocol

Seven stages. Zero compromise. Every track shipped to label spec.

01 / SIGNAL

Source Lock

Pre-production audit. We isolate the obsession — the single sonic fingerprint a track must defend at every later stage. No fingerprint, no protocol.

02 / FRAME

Arrangement Audit

The 30/60/90-second test. Tracks are restructured against supervisor scan-points: hook before 0:30, breakdown before 0:60, climax before 1:30.

03 / RENDER

Stems Discipline

The OCD eight-stem export standard — drums, bass, harmonic, lead, pad, FX, vocal lead, vocal bed. Naming, headroom, sample rate, and bit depth fixed to spec.

04 / MASTER

Loudness Targets

-14 LUFS streaming, -23 LUFS broadcast, -1 dBTP ceiling. The reference master suite — three calibrated comparisons per genre — ships in the Vault.

05 / META

Identity Stack

ISRC, ISWC, PRO registration, writer splits, instrumental and vocal alt-mixes, BPM, key, mood tags. The metadata sheet template clears 80% of library intake forms unchanged.

06 / PITCH

One-Sheet Engine

Supervisor-format one-sheets: hook description, reference cues, vibe tags, link tree, clearance status. Fill the template, export PDF, send.

07 / LOCK

Frequency Rights

License-agnostic contract scaffolds: non-exclusive sync, festival cue sheets, brand-spot terms. Built from the OCD Records licensing book.
