## Winning Product Structure — Signing Filter™ Report

- **Name:** *Signing Filter™ — the 12-Axis Demo Audit* (proprietary mechanism: the 12 axes and the neural+human blend are yours; nobody else scores in this shape).
- **Contents delivered per report:**
  1. **The 12-Axis Score Card** — arrangement, hook density, mix translation, low-end control, stereo image, dynamic range, sound-design signature, tempo/key coherence, transitions, sonic identity vs. catalog, sync usability, artist-project consistency. Each 0–10 with one-line justification.
  2. **Neural Layer** — automated readout (loudness LUFS, true peak, spectral tilt, correlation, key detection) from a scripted analysis pass, embedded as a labeled chart.
  3. **A&R Layer** — 300–500 words in your voice: what would make this signable, what kills it, one reference artist already in the OCD orbit for comparison.
  4. **The Verdict** — one of four: *Sign-track candidate / Catalog-adjacent / Rework recommended / Not the fit* — plus the single highest-leverage fix.
  5. **Bonus artifact** — a 60-sec Loom of the track playing while your notes scroll (recorded once per report; the only manual step besides writing the A&R paragraph).
- **Delivery mechanism (no live presence required):**
  - Existing `submit.html` form → adds a $-tier ("Priority Filter — 48h response").
  - Stripe checkout → Airtable/Notion intake → your review queue.
  - Report templated in a Notion doc → exported to branded PDF → auto-emailed via a Zapier/Make step.
  - You touch it *only* when writing the A&R paragraph + recording the Loom (~20 min per report).
- **Price point:** **$149 solo / $249 with 1 rework re-audit.**
  Rationale: producers already spend $100+ on mastering they can't evaluate; this tells them *whether mastering is even the problem*. Above the impulse threshold, below the "gotta think about it" threshold. Bundle re-audit lifts AOV without adding live time.
- **What makes it irreplaceable:** the 12-axis rubric is *your* framework, published nowhere else. The neural+A&R blend cannot be replicated by an AI-only tool (no taste) or a human-only consultant (no consistency), and no other label sells its internal filter to the outside world — every producer who buys this is training themselves on your label's actual signing criteria, which makes their *next* submission to OCD dramatically more likely to convert. The product feeds the roster.
