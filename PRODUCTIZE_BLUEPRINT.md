## Winning Product Structure — *The OCS Kit™*

- **Name:** **The OCS Kit** — *Obsession → Catalog System* (proprietary
  4-stage method: **Capture · Curate · Codify · Catalog**).
- **Contents:**
  1. `OCS-Playbook.pdf` — the 4-stage method, decision trees, and the
     "48-hour Release" checklist.
  2. `OCS-Templates.zip` — Ableton + FL project skeletons pre-wired for
     stem export, LUFS targets, and metadata baking.
  3. `Catalog-Schema.json` — the exact metadata spec the OCD Records
     catalog uses (BPM, mood, sync tags, moral rights flags, AI-provenance
     field). Drop-in for Airtable / Notion / any DAM.
  4. `License-Pack.docx` — 6 negotiated-and-lawyered contract templates
     (sync, sample, stem, exclusive, non-exclusive, AI-training opt-out).
  5. `The Neural Ghost Case Study` — walk-through of one release from raw
     compulsion to first licensed placement.
- **Delivery:** Gumroad / Lemon Squeezy checkout → auto-issued download
  link + Discord role. Zero founder touch. Refund policy is automatic.
- **What makes it irreplaceable:** the metadata schema and license pack
  are the assets the buyer would otherwise spend $3–5k on a lawyer +
  librarian to produce. The named 4-stage method (OCS) gives buyers
  language they carry back to their studio and share — turns the kit into
  its own distribution.
- **Price point:** **$149 launch → $249 standing.** Rationale: below the
  cost of a single hour of an entertainment lawyer's time, above
  impulse-buy threshold so buyers actually use it (usage → testimonials →
  referrals). A $997 "Studio" tier adds a 30-min onboarding call —
  optional, capped, and *not* required for the product to work.

## Distribution Channel — matches the existing platform

**The OCD Records site itself.** The catalog + `submit.html` funnel already
attracts the exact avatar (compulsive producers looking to release). Ship
the kit as a soft-sell block on the submissions page and a dedicated
`/kit.html` route linked from the licensing footer. Do **not** open a new
channel (no TikTok/newsletter build from zero). Existing SEO surface for
"AI music licensing" and "generative music catalog" is the wedge.

Secondary: one email per accepted-but-declined submission
("here's the system we use — build your own catalog"). This converts
rejection into revenue without new audience-building.

## Week 1 Build Roadmap

1. **Draft `OCS-Playbook.pdf` outline + the Catalog-Schema.json.** These
   two artifacts are the spine — everything else references them. Ship
   the schema first because it's the smallest thing with irreplaceable
   value (2–3 focused sessions).
2. **Stand up `/kit.html` on the existing site** using the same
   Tailwind/Archivo/Manrope system as `licensing.html`. Wire the
   Gumroad/Lemon Squeezy checkout. No new stack, no new brand.
3. **Recruit 5 beta buyers from the current submissions inbox** at $49
   in exchange for a testimonial + one gap-report. Use their reports to
   finalize the License-Pack contracts before public launch.
