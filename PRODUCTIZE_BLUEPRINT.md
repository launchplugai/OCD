## Winning Product Structure

### Name
**The Sonic Architecture Audit™** — powered by the **SAP-5** rubric (Signal, Arrangement, Presence, Emotion, Placement).

### The proprietary mechanism (SAP-5)

Five pillars, four bands each (Reject / Refine / Ready / Reference). Score is the sum; the report is the pillar-level breakdown, not just the number.

1. **S — Signal.** Mix cleanliness, headroom, mono compatibility, artifact-free master.
2. **A — Arrangement.** Tension curve, section economy, entry/exit fidelity.
3. **P — Presence.** Sonic identity — does this sound like *someone*, or like every other track in the genre?
4. **E — Emotion.** Named affect and a moment where the track earns it. If you can't name the feeling in one word, it isn't there.
5. **P — Placement.** Where it could actually live: sync/film/game/streaming playlist. A track without a home doesn't get licensed.

The rubric doubles as marketing collateral. Publish the one-pager for free. The paid audit is *your ear on their track against the rubric*, with specific remediation notes.

### Contents (what the buyer gets)

- **Verdict PDF (2–4 pages):**
  - Overall score + one-line label decision (`Release-Ready` / `Refine → Resubmit` / `Not This One`).
  - Pillar-by-pillar breakdown with specific timestamps ("2:14 — snare masking vocal presence; try -3dB @ 2.5kHz").
  - Top 3 fixes ranked by ROI. Nothing else — no throat-clearing.
  - One suggested placement lane (sync/playlist/label pitch).
- **Bonus for `Release-Ready` verdicts:** priority slot in the OCD Records A&R queue — the audit becomes a paid fast-lane into the free submission funnel.
- **Public rubric:** SAP-5 one-pager, freely downloadable, credited to OCD Records. This is the marketing engine.

### Delivery mechanism (how it reaches buyers without you)

The current `submit.html` becomes a two-tier funnel:

1. **Free Transmission** (existing flow, unchanged). ~6 week review, no guarantee.
2. **Audit Track** ($79 single / $199 three-pack / $499 Label Path pack — 5 audits + submission template + licensing brief). Stripe checkout inline. 24-hour turnaround SLA.

Fulfillment loop:
- Stripe checkout → Zap/webhook → intake row in a Notion or Airtable base with the WAV link + brief.
- You (or a trained engineer from the Studios roster) score against the rubric using a locked template. Ninety-percent of the report is the template; ten percent is the ear.
- Locked template exports to PDF, auto-emails the buyer.
- Every 5th audit gets sampled for QA against a baseline.

The *asset* being sold is the rubric + the label's name on the verdict. Neither requires you to be online.

### What makes it irreplaceable

- **The verdict is signed by a working label** that is already licensing tracks at $299–$799. Nobody else in the "get feedback on your track" market has that credential stapled to the certificate.
- **The rubric is proprietary** (SAP-5, trademarked language, published as marketing). Competitors giving generic mix feedback don't have a named methodology anchored to a label roster.
- **The fast-lane into A&R** — a `Release-Ready` verdict skips the free-submission queue. That's a benefit no third-party service can offer.
- **The output is decisive.** Every other feedback service gives you a paragraph and hedges. Yours gives a single-line label decision.

### Price point + rationale

- **$79 single audit.** Under the psychological "yes on Friday night after a session" threshold; roughly the price of a decent mic cable. Filters serious producers from time-wasters.
- **$199 three-pack** (best value, most bought). Producers work in EPs, not singles — three tracks matches the natural unit of work.
- **$499 Label Path pack.** 5 audits + submission template + a one-page licensing brief. The step-up to the eventual course/community.
- Rationale on the ladder: single is discovery, three-pack is the sweet spot, Label Path is the qualification asset for the next product. Ratio 1 : 2.5 : 6.3 mirrors the classic tripwire ladder without leaving money on the table.

At the $199 median, at 20 audits/week (a modest goal from existing inbound), the product does ~$200k/yr at >90% margin with no live presence required.

## Week 1 Build Roadmap (3 tasks to start immediately)

1. **Write the SAP-5 rubric as a one-page public artifact.** Five pillars × four bands, with one-sentence definitions and a scoring example against an existing catalog release (e.g. *Entropy 001*). This is the marketing engine and the QA anchor — everything else references it. Ship as `sap-5.html` styled to match the site.
2. **Convert `submit.html` into a two-tier funnel.** Keep the existing free flow. Add a parallel *Audit Track* column with Stripe Checkout links for the three tiers, the 24h SLA, and the "priority into A&R" bonus. No backend required for MVP — Stripe hosted checkout + a form redirect is enough.
3. **Build the verdict PDF template + fulfillment SOP.** Locked template (Notion → PDF export, or a Google Doc template with a merge script) with the five pillar sections, the decision line, timestamped-fixes table, and placement suggestion. Write the SOP as a 10-step checklist an engineer from the Studios roster could execute. Do the first three audits yourself, unpaid, on catalog tracks to calibrate the rubric.

Everything past Week 1 (Stripe automation, Zapier fulfillment, roster crossposts, the Academy roadmap) waits until those three exist.

## Reject-list (what this blueprint deliberately rules out)

- **Live cohort teaching / 1:1 coaching.** Fails the "sells at 3 a.m." test.
- **A generic "get feedback on your track" service.** Fails the "named framework" rule; commoditized.
- **A launch on a new platform (TikTok, YouTube, Substack).** Violates the "no zero-audience channels" rule when `submit.html` is already receiving intent traffic.
- **Bundling the audit into the free submission flow.** Would train producers to expect free feedback and collapse the price anchor.
