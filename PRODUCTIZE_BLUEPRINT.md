## Winning Product Structure

### Name
**The OCD Protocol Playbook** — the Signal//Noise Ratio, released.

### Contents (v1, ships in 30 days)
- **The Protocol PDF** (~40 pp): the four Signal//Noise axes, scoring
  rubric (0–5 each; 14+ passes A&R triage), and three worked examples
  scoring real catalog tracks (Entropy 001, plus two more from
  `catalog.html`).
- **Reference Audio Pack**: 12 A/B pairs (accepted vs rejected) — one
  trio per Signal//Noise axis, plus three "borderline" cases so the ear
  training isn't binary.
- **Mastering Target Templates**: LUFS/TP/tonal curve `.wav` references
  and matching iZotope/FabFilter/Ozone preset files — the actual targets
  OCD's masters land on.
- **The Submission Self-Audit**: a one-page checklist that scores a demo
  against the grid before it goes to any label (not just OCD).

### Delivery (no live presence)
- Sold through Gumroad or Stripe Checkout → instant `.zip` + PDF download.
- Product page wired into the existing site as `playbook.html` and added
  to `NavigationHeader` alongside Licensing.
- Buyer email → dripped 5-part onboarding (day 0/1/3/7/14) that teaches
  one Signal//Noise axis per touch, with a soft push to submit (or license).
- Auto-generated OCD-branded certificate when the buyer self-scores 14+
  on their own track — costs nothing, spreads on social.

### What makes it irreplaceable
- **Proprietary methodology**: the Signal//Noise Ratio is the label's
  own internal grid. No competitor can source it.
- **Proof-by-catalog**: the worked examples score OCD's own releases.
  Every buyer is auditing the same tracks a paying licensee just bought.
- **Two-sided pull**: the Playbook is a filter for the label's
  submission funnel. Buyers are pre-qualified for future releases; the
  label saves A&R hours. The product improves the business it came from.

### Price point
**$79 launch / $129 standard.** Rationale:
- Under $100 clears impulse-buy on a producer's PayPal.
- Above $49 signals it's not a $19 sample pack — it's a methodology.
- Anchors cleanly against the existing $299 Standard License tier —
  Playbook is the on-ramp; licensing is the escalator.
- Margin at $79 with ~$3 processor fees ≈ 96%. Passes.
