// 01 Why It's Irreplaceable

Every artifact in the Vault passes the same four-stage pipeline OCD Records uses to develop its roster. It is the reason the catalog sounds like nothing in a stock library — and the reason it clears instantly.

Stage 01

Source

Only roster-grade masters enter the chain. Obsession over loudness — selected for sonic identity, not trend.

Stage 02

Stem-Split

Each track is decomposed into deploy-ready stems — drums, bass, texture, lead — so it bends to your edit.

Stage 03

Pre-Clear
