// 06 FAQ

Common interference

Is this a course with videos I'll never watch?

No. It's a kit. A PDF rubric, real stems, real templates, real presets. You can run the entire OCD Method on your next track in one studio session.

Do I get signed if I score 72+?

No guarantee — signing is also about timing and roster fit. But a 72+ track gets a real A&R listen instead of being skipped at 11 seconds. That's the bar.

What genres does this work for?

Electronic, ambient, cinematic, dark techno, avant-garde. The rubric translates to anything with a producer at the center of the workflow. If you make pop vocals, this isn't for you.

Are the stems actually clearable?

Yes. Royalty-free for unlimited use in your own productions, including commercial releases. Resale or redistribution of the raw stems is not permitted.
